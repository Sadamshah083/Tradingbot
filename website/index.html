How it works

Three parts, all yours to run. Nobody else touches your money or your broker keys.

1 · TRADINGVIEW

The strategy

A Pine Script v6 strategy you paste into TradingView. Anchored VWAP with standard-deviation bands; a bar that opens outside the band and closes back inside is the signal. Stop = signal bar ± ATR buffer, target = VWAP + 1 SD. Levels are printed on the chart at signal time and never repaint.

2 · WEBHOOK BOT

The bridge

A small server (Python, Docker-ready) that receives TradingView's alert, checks a passphrase, journals every signal and, in AUTO mode, places the order with stop and target at OANDA (gold) or Binance futures (BTC). MANUAL mode only notifies you on Telegram. Switch modes from a web panel.

3 · YOUR BROKER

The execution

Orders go straight from your server to your broker account with the stop-loss and take-profit attached, so they keep protecting the trade even if your server or TradingView goes offline. Paper mode is the default; you switch to live when you decide.

Where it does not work

Bitcoin on 5m–1h charts lost money after exchange fees in every setting tested. Gold on 5m/15m had too few signals in the available data to be trusted. For Bitcoin, the included EMA toolkit on the daily chart (36% win rate, profit factor 1.76 over 15 years) is the right tool.

How to read this honestly

79 trades is a modest sample. Numbers on data the settings never saw (out-of-sample) are lower than the headline and are the ones to expect. Live trading adds spread, slippage and human error. Run it in paper mode for weeks before risking money, and never without the stop.

Questions people ask

Is it 100% accurate?

No, and nothing is. In testing the gold 1h strategy won about 8 of 10 trades and lost 2. A losing streak of several trades will happen. The stop-loss on every trade is what keeps a losing streak survivable; the win rate is not what makes it profitable — the ratio between average win and average loss is.

Does the bot trade my account for me?

Only if you switch it to AUTO mode, on your own server, with your own broker API keys. We never hold your funds or your keys. In MANUAL mode it just sends you the signal.

What do I need?

A TradingView account on the Essential plan or higher (webhook alerts) with two-factor authentication enabled; a broker account (OANDA for gold, Binance futures for BTC), demo first; and either a $4–7/month VPS or a PC that stays on. Setup takes about an hour with the guide.

Which markets and timeframes?

Gold (XAUUSD) on the 1-hour chart is the tested configuration. The 4-hour chart works with fewer signals. Bitcoin intraday is not supported; use the daily EMA toolkit for BTC.

Can I change the settings?

Yes — every parameter is an input with a tooltip, and the research toolkit lets you test any change on the historical data before using it.
