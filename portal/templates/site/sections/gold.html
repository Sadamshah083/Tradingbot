XAUUSD automation built around testing

Gold can move quickly around economic events, changing liquidity and active market sessions. Configuration, testing and risk controls matter.

SamBot combines supported TradingView gold signals with webhook automation, historical strategy testing, paper execution, configurable position risk, stop and target handling, activity records, and supported MT5 connectivity.

Not GLD. The gold bot targets XAUUSD. GLD is a US gold ETF with different hours and costs. Research candles may use a GC=F proxy — never generate signals on one gold product and execute them on another. See the Strategy Lab.
