Why test Bitcoin automation carefully?

A continuously operating market can generate signals outside the hours when you would normally be monitoring a screen. Automation makes consistency possible — but configuration mistakes can also be repeated automatically. That is why SamBot combines automation with account limits, paper testing and recorded activity.
