Paper trading

Test the automation before putting capital at risk

Sambot paper mode allows you to observe the trading workflow with simulated execution. No connected trading funds are required.

Why paper trading matters

A backtest answers: “How would these rules have behaved against historical data and assumptions?” Paper trading answers: “How is this automation behaving now as new signals arrive?” You need both perspectives.

Paper results are still simulations

Paper execution cannot perfectly recreate live spreads, slippage, gaps, latency, liquidity or broker behaviour. Its value is revealing configuration and strategy issues without exposing live capital.

What paper mode lets you examine

Signal behaviour

Confirm that TradingView is sending the signals you expect.

Position sizing

Check whether configured risk settings produce the intended simulated trade sizes.

Rejections

See why a signal was blocked when it violated a configured rule.

Stops and targets

Observe how protective rules are represented in the workflow.

Strategy consistency

Compare new paper results with historical expectations.
