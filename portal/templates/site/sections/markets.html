Markets currently supported by Sambot

Sambot focuses on a deliberately limited set of markets rather than claiming to automate everything.

METALS · SESSIONS

Gold / XAUUSD

Gold is traded within market sessions rather than continuously. Supported gold workflows use defined trading sessions, TradingView signals, configurable account controls and recorded activity.

  • XAUUSD strategy research
  • Gold backtesting
  • Paper-mode testing
  • MT5 workflow
  • Risk controls
Explore Gold Automation
CRYPTO · 24/7

Bitcoin

Bitcoin markets operate continuously, creating a different automation environment from session-based markets. Supported BTC workflows include paper testing, account controls and recorded signal activity.

  • BTC strategy research
  • Historical testing
  • Binance integration
  • Paper mode
  • Risk management
