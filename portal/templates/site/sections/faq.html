Sambot Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about Sambot, TradingView automation, paper trading, backtesting, Binance, MetaTrader 5, risk controls and pricing.

What is Sambot?

Sambot is trading-automation software that connects supported TradingView signals with a configurable workflow including validation, account controls, paper trading and supported execution integrations.

Does Sambot guarantee profits?

No. Trading results cannot be guaranteed. Historical backtests and simulations do not predict future results.

Can I test Sambot without trading live?

Yes. Paper mode is designed for testing the signal and automation workflow using simulated execution.

Does Sambot hold my money?

No. Sambot is software. Funds remain with the account provider you choose to connect.

What is a TradingView webhook?

A webhook allows a TradingView alert to send information to an external application when an alert triggers. Sambot uses a webhook as part of its supported TradingView automation workflow.

Does Sambot support Binance?

Sambot currently supports an integration workflow for Binance Futures. Availability and functionality should always be checked against the current product documentation.

Does Sambot support MetaTrader 5?

Sambot supports an MT5 workflow through its bridge architecture.

Can Sambot trade gold?

Sambot currently includes supported research and automation workflows for gold/XAUUSD.

Can Sambot automate Bitcoin?

Sambot currently includes supported Bitcoin-oriented automation workflows.

What risk controls are available?

Depending on the current product version, supported controls can include position risk, daily loss limits, active-position limits, exposure controls, losing-streak limits, stop loss, take profit, break-even, trailing stops and an emergency stop.

Are backtests live results?

No. A backtest is a historical simulation based on historical data and defined assumptions.

Why are losing trades shown?

Because a strategy cannot be evaluated responsibly using winning trades alone.

How much does Sambot cost?

Refer to the current Pricing page for the latest software-access and signal pricing.

Is Sambot financial advice?

No. Sambot provides software, research tools and informational functionality. It does not provide personalised financial advice.
