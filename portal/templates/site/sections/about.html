Why Sambot exists

Too much trading software is marketed backwards. The promise comes first. The evidence comes later. Sambot takes a different approach.

We believe automated trading software should make it easier to examine what a system is actually doing. That means displaying losing trades, explaining how signals move through the system, making paper testing easily accessible, showing the assumptions behind historical simulations, keeping controls visible, and being clear that no backtest can guarantee what happens next.

Our principles

Test before trust

Use research and simulated workflows before putting capital at risk.

Show the losses too

A strategy cannot be understood by studying only its successful trades.

Keep controls visible

Automation should follow defined limits rather than remove them.

Keep the user in control

Your account configuration and trading decisions remain your responsibility.

Never promise returns

Markets are uncertain. Software does not change that.

Tools, not guarantees

Sambot provides tools for research, automation and monitoring — not guaranteed investment outcomes.
