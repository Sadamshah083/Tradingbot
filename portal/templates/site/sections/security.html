Security & risk

What protects you

ENCRYPTED KEYSBroker credentials are encrypted at rest and only decrypted by the trading engine.
NO WITHDRAWALSUse API keys with trading permission only; the bot never needs withdrawal rights.
SIGNED WEBHOOKSEvery TradingView alert must carry a secret; Stripe webhooks are signature-verified.
AUDIT TRAILSettings changes, kill switches, orders and rejections are logged per account.

Trading involves substantial risk of loss. Software, connections, exchanges and brokers all fail sometimes; automated orders can fill at unexpected prices. Keep a stop at the broker, monitor any live system, and never trade money you cannot afford to lose. See the disclaimer and risk disclosure.
