Performance

Performance should be inspected, not advertised as certainty

Trading performance cannot be reduced to one percentage. Sambot presents strategy research with the data needed to examine the result in context.

What we publish

Historical date range & number of trades

Winning trades, losing trades & profit factor

Drawdown, assumed costs & trade list

Historical performance is not live performance

Backtests simulate previous conditions. Real markets introduce changing spreads, slippage, latency, liquidity, gaps, data differences and execution behaviour. Future results can be materially different.

Our goal is not to ask you to trust a headline percentage. Review the methodology. Review the trade history. Look at the losing periods. Then observe the strategy in paper mode.
