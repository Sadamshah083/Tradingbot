MetaTrader 5

TradingView and MetaTrader 5 automation

Sambot supports an MT5-based workflow using a bridge that operates alongside a MetaTrader 5 terminal.

How the MT5 workflow works

01

Alert

TradingView generates an alert.

02

Validate

Sambot receives and validates it.

03

Rules

Configured account rules are evaluated.

04

Bridge

The supported instruction is sent through the MT5 bridge.

05

Broker

MetaTrader handles the broker-side environment.

06

Record

Sambot records the workflow activity.

Why an MT5 bridge?

MetaTrader environments operate differently from direct exchange APIs. The bridge connects the Sambot workflow with the supported MT5 environment while keeping TradingView as the signal source.
