TradingView

TradingView webhook automation without the black box

TradingView can generate highly configurable alerts from prices, indicators and strategies. SamBot receives supported alerts and processes them through validation, account rules and an auditable workflow.

What is a TradingView webhook bot?

Software that receives an alert from TradingView through a webhook and performs a predefined action with that information. SamBot uses this to connect supported strategy alerts with paper trading and supported execution integrations.

Why use a webhook workflow?

A webhook removes the need to manually copy information from every TradingView alert. More importantly, SamBot can apply the same validation and account rules each time a supported alert arrives.

Your TradingView workflow

TradingView Strategy→Webhook Alert→SamBot Validation→Risk Rules→Paper or Supported Execution→Trade Record

Built for testing

Do not assume that an alert appearing correctly on a chart means an automated workflow will behave identically. Paper testing lets you inspect the complete chain before considering another environment.
