      About the “80% win rate” goal

      We can measure whether a version reaches 80%. We cannot responsibly design a bot that guarantees it. A strategy can win often and still lose money if losers are large. Useful measures: expectancy, drawdown, profit factor, costs, and held-out sample size.

      How we tested

      Rules were fixed first. History was split at 2024-01-01 (in-sample before, held-out after). Costs are modelled. Searching hundreds of thresholds until one chart hits 80% is overfitting — we do not do that here.

      Risk rules for both bots

      Allocation

      Max ~10% of equity per instrument (configurable). Gold and BTC limits stay separate, plus one combined account cap.

      Protective stop

      2× ATR(14) below entry is the test default. A stop limits the intended exit; it cannot guarantee the fill price when markets gap.

      Reject / pause

      Reject if candle is missing/stale, funds are insufficient, an order is pending, or the daily loss limit is hit. Pause new entries after 3 consecutive losses until review.
