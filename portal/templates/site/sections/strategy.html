Rules you can read, limits you control

VWAP Reversal: after price stretches beyond the 2.5-standard-deviation band around VWAP and closes back inside, a mean-reversion signal is issued with a stop beyond the extreme and a target at the band. Confirmed on bar close, so it never repaints. The risk engine wraps every signal:

RISK / TRADEPosition sized so a stop-out costs a fixed % of equity (0.1 - 5%).
DAILY LOSSTrading stops for the day once the limit is reached.

LOSING STREAKPause after N consecutive losses.
STOP / TARGETFrom the signal or fixed % / risk-reward, placed with the order.
BREAK-EVENStop moves to entry after a configurable number of R.
TRAILING STOPFollows price at a % distance once in profit.
