Risk management

Put your trading limits into the automation

These controls cannot remove market risk. They help make your selected limits part of the system instead of something you have to remember after an alert appears.

Risk per trade

Define the maximum configured account risk permitted for an individual workflow.

Daily loss limit

Set a threshold after which new trading activity can be blocked for the configured period.

Maximum open positions

Limit the number of simultaneous active positions.

Exposure control

Reduce the chance of unintentionally stacking multiple positions beyond your configured limits.

Losing-streak controls

Pause new activity after a configured sequence of losing results.

Stop loss

Use predefined protective exit logic according to the supported configuration.

Take profit

Define supported profit-target behaviour.

Break-even management

Configure supported logic that adjusts a stop after predefined conditions are reached.

Trailing stop

Configure supported trailing behaviour once the required conditions have been met.

Emergency stop

Stop new automated activity when you want the system paused.

Risk controls do not make trading safe

Markets can gap. Connections can fail. Orders may fill differently than expected. Software can experience errors. Risk controls are safeguards — not guarantees.
