Paper trading & backtests

Test Your Strategy Before Live Trading

Two different ways to examine a strategy. Neither guarantees future live results, so use them together.

Check Your Workflow With Paper Trading

Paper trading lets you examine simulated trading activity without placing live orders. Use it to check incoming alerts, review position sizing, and see how your risk rules affect the workflow.

Treat this stage as a chance to find setup problems. Simulated results can differ from live trading because real execution involves changing prices, fees, liquidity, and slippage.

Explore Paper Trading →

Review Historical Strategy Backtests

Backtesting helps you examine how a strategy would have behaved using historical data and stated assumptions. Review the trade list, costs, and periods of loss alongside the overall result.

A backtest is a research tool, not evidence of future profitability. Consider it together with paper testing before deciding whether a strategy suits live use.

Risk management

Set Trading Limits Before Automation Starts

SamBot includes configurable risk controls such as daily loss limits and maximum open positions. Choose limits that reflect your own trading plan, then check their behavior in paper mode. Risk controls can support a consistent process, but they cannot eliminate losses or guarantee execution at a particular price.

Daily profit target

Pause new activity after a configured daily goal.

Daily loss limit

Block new trades when the daily loss threshold is hit.

Max open trades

Cap how many positions can run at once.

Trailing stop

Move protection as price moves in your favour.
