Why SamBot

Trading Automation Built Around Your Rules

Automation should not mean handing control to an opaque system. SamBot brings signals, configurable rules, risk controls and supported execution environments into one workflow that can be tested and reviewed.

01

Connect Your Signals

Each account gets its own TradingView webhook address and secret. When an alert fires, TradingView posts the message to that address and SamBot validates it before anything else happens. Unsupported or malformed messages are rejected and logged.

02

Control the Risk

Set your rules before a signal arrives: risk per trade, a daily loss limit, the number of open trades, a trailing stop and an emergency stop. Every signal is checked against them, and a signal that breaks a rule is skipped rather than traded.

The workflow

From Signal to Execution

Every alert passes through the same five stages. Select a stage to see what happens there.

    1. TradingView alertYour strategy or indicator fires an alert.
    2. WebhookThe alert is posted to your personal webhook address.
    3. SamBotThe message and your account are validated.
    4. Risk rulesYour limits decide whether the signal may trade.
    5. ExecutionPaper fill, or a supported MT5 or Binance workflow.

    TradingView integration

    Turn TradingView Alerts Into an Automated Workflow

    TradingView can act as the signal source for supported SamBot workflows. Alerts are delivered through webhooks and processed through your configured rules before execution.

    Because the same checks run on every alert, you can see exactly why a signal was traded, skipped or rejected. TradingView limits webhook alerts to certain plans, so check your plan first.

    Explore TradingView Automation

    Paper mode

    Test Your Strategy Before Live Trading

    Paper mode runs your real alerts through the same rules with simulated fills, so you can find problems while nothing is at stake. It will not match live trading exactly, since spreads, slippage and fills differ, but it shows whether the workflow does what you expect.

    • Test how signals behave
    • Validate your configuration
    • Review the execution workflow
    • Understand your risk settings
    • Spot configuration problems
    • Observe the strategy over time

    Risk management

    Automation With Risk Controls Built In

    Automation does not remove trading risk. What it can do is apply the rules you chose, the same way, to every signal. Set the limits below before you connect anything, and test how they behave in paper mode.

    Controls help you enforce a plan. They cannot guarantee a particular fill price or protect an account from losses.
